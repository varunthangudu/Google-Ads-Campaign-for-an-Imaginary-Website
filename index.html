Questions we get asked

How long does a deep clean take?

About 190 minutes for a 2BHK with a team of three. Larger homes and move-out cleans run four to six hours. We'll tell you the expected duration when we confirm your slot.

Do I need to be at home during the clean?

Someone should be present at the start and at the final walkthrough. In between you're free to step out.

Do you bring your own equipment and chemicals?

Yes — vacuum, scrubber, extraction machine and all consumables come with the team. We only need access to water and a power point.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell the supervisor before paying and they'll redo it on the spot. If you notice something later, our seven-day re-clean covers it.

How do I pay?

After the clean, by UPI, card or cash. No advance and no deposit.
